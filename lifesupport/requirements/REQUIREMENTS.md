# LIFE-CORE-002 requirements baseline

Source of truth: `data/registry.json`, requirements `LREQ-001` through `LREQ-024`.
Each requirement includes statement, allocated components, verification method, acceptance criteria and lifecycle status.
All domain requirements currently cover *registry representation*, not actual life-support performance.

## Future engineering requirements (NOT baselined)
- Air composition, ventilation and contamination limits based on occupancy and operating environment.
- Potable water quality, sanitation and reserve capacity.
- Thermal comfort, humidity, heat rejection and extreme-climate performance.
- Fire detection, evacuation, containment, alarms and safe states.
- Medical infrastructure, food hygiene and waste handling.
- Failure modes, independence, common-cause failures, minimum autonomy and manual operation.

No safety setpoints or autonomy duration are asserted until a hazard analysis, applicable standards and operating design basis are approved.
